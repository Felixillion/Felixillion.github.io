Net value = bonus points × your point value − annual fee (+ cashback, and travel credits if ticked). Cost per point = what you pay per bonus point after cashback and credits. Neither includes points from everyday spending, interest, foreign-transaction fees, insurance or lounge access. Year-2 bonuses usually need the second annual fee and sometimes more spending.     Before you apply

    1. Not financial advice. This is general information only. Read the bank's Target Market Determination and terms, and check the offer on the bank's site before applying — offers change without notice.

    2. Pay the balance in full every month. Interest wipes out the value of points quickly — Moneysmart: credit cards.

    3. Applications show on your credit report. Many applications in a short time can affect your credit score. You can check yours free — see Moneysmart: credit reports.

    4. Only cards that earn Qantas or Velocity points directly (or convert them automatically) are listed; flexible-points cards (e.g. Amex Membership Rewards) and business cards aren't included.

    5. Some “Apply” links are referral links: I may earn a commission if you're approved, at no cost to you. It doesn't affect the order (sorted by value).
