Earning tables
Status Credits are a fixed amount per flight, set by the fare type and either the flight's distance band or (for many flights from Australia) a route-specific table — from Qantas's official earning tables. Domestic distance bands: 0–750, 751–1,500 and 1,501+ miles. On domestic flights Economy Flex earns exactly 2× Sale/Saver, Business 4× and Business Flex about 4.5–4.75×. Distances are great-circle estimates; routes marked ⚠ are within 30 miles of a band edge — confirm with the Qantas earning calculator.
