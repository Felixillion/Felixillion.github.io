💡 Multi-Leg Advantage — Reroutes & Status Runs
When you book via a connecting city, each segment earns status credits separately. A SYD→MEL direct earns 20 SC. The same journey via BNE earns 40 SC — for the same price. The "Status Run" category shows full day-return trips optimised purely for SC accumulation.

How qualification works (Nov 2026): Status Credits count within your membership year (not a rolling 12 months), and every tier above Bronze also needs at least 4 eligible Qantas/Jetstar flights. Retention thresholds rise to the full earn thresholds from late 2027, when the 50-SC Loyalty Bonuses also end. Points Club / Points Club Plus close at the end of 2026; from then, ground-partner activity can earn up to 140 SC a year and 50% of excess SC roll over (capped by tier).
